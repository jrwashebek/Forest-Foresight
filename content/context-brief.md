# Context brief: US forestry, November 2026

*Reviewed 2026-11-14; figures as reported by linked sources.*

## Federal: Forest Service
- **Workforce.** About 16–20% of staff left in 2025, mostly via deferred resignation (~5,900–7,000; counts vary). Firefighter hiring recovered (~9,700 by March 2026); support roles lag. [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/09/03/us-forest-service-restructuring-plan/), [GAO](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107993/index.html), [NPR/HPPR](https://www.hppr.org/npr-news/2026-05-04/trump-administration-falls-behind-on-wildfire-prevention-with-risky-fire-season-ahead)
- **Reorganization.** Nine regional offices closing, headquarters to Salt Lake City, 15 state directors. Research-site closures cut from 57 to nearly two dozen. Western governors support; retirees and unions warn of brain drain and politicization. *Contested:* relocations (under 300 per the Chief; up to 1,900 per whistleblowers). [GovExec](https://www.govexec.com/management/2026/04/forest-service-move-hq-out-dc-shutter-regional-offices-sweeping-overhaul/412566/), [Daily Montanan](https://dailymontanan.com/2026/07/06/chief-tom-schultz-details-defends-largest-forest-service-reorganization-in-a-century/)
- **Fire governance.** Interior launched the U.S. Wildland Fire Service (January 2026). Absorbing Forest Service fire needs Congress, which funds them separately and wants a feasibility study. Interior presumes full suppression, limiting late-season prescribed fire. [FNN](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/reorganization/2026/02/interior-dept-blazes-ahead-on-unified-wildland-firefighting-agency-without-congress-endorsing-plans/), [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/05/17/nx-s1-5777660/forest-service-wildfire-safety-prevention-trump-administration)
- **Budget.** FY2026 enacted ~$8.6B. FY2027 request ~$2.1B (fire moved to Interior; National Forest System −24%; most research and state, private and Tribal forestry zeroed); House committee bill ~$8.8B. Stopgap to Dec 11. 2025 reconciliation clawed back unspent IRA forestry funds ($1.5B urban forestry). [CRS](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R49292.html), [NAISMA](https://naisma.org/2025/09/22/how-obbb-impacts-invasive-species-and-forest-resilience-programs/)
- **Policy.** Timber targets up 25%; FY2025 sales ~2.95B board feet, limited by staffing. Roadless rescission (~45M acres): comments closed Oct 6; no final decision found. December 2025 grant terms (DEI, immigration, climate) stalled wildfire grants; 20 states and D.C. sued. Fix Our Forests Act awaits a Senate vote. [Outdoor Alliance](https://www.outdooralliance.org/blog/2026/8/18/administration-takes-final-steps-to-roll-back-roadless-rule-with-30-day-comment-period), [Deseret News](https://deseret.com/environment/2026/11/13/fire-seasons-are-becoming-fire-years-will-congress-act)
- **Congress.** As of Nov 14, 9:10am PT, AP's count shows 256 House seats won by Democrats and 169 by Republicans; Senate totals are 55 Democrats (including 2 who caucus with them) and 44 Republicans. In those results, 10 House seats are counted as won for no party and 1 Senate seat as won or held over for no party, without saying why or which way they will go. Counts may have changed. New Congress: January 2027.

## States
- States gain a formal channel via state directors but lean on federal money (about a third of Vermont's forestry budget). California's wildfire-resilience spending fell from ~$1.1B (2021–22) to ~$620M (2025–26). [Vermont FPR](https://legislature.vermont.gov/Documents/2026/Workgroups/House%20Agriculture/Agency%20of%20Natural%20Resources/Department%20of%20Forests,%20Parks,%20and%20Recreation/W~Oliver%20Pierson~Effects%20of%20USFS%20Re-organization~5-12-2026.pdf), [California LAO](https://lao.ca.gov/handouts/resources/2026/State-Wildfire-Resilience-Funding-051326.pdf)

## Tribal
- Co-stewardship continues across administrations (Cow Creek Umpqua, 155,000 acres; a Black Elk Wilderness MOU with the Great Sioux Nation). About 900 Tribal firefighters work alongside Interior's fire service. [Tribal Business News](https://tribalbusinessnews.com/sections/energy/15500-cow-creek-umpqua-signs-155-000-acre-forest-co-stewardship-agreement-with-u-s-forest-service), [Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/uncategorized/native-news-weekly-march-22-2026-d-c-briefs), [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/05/02/us-wildland-fire-service-first-chief/)

## Private forests and markets
- Private land supplies most harvest (national forests ~4%). Pulpwood is weak amid mill closures; some sawmills open. Canadian softwood faces ~45% combined duties; 50% tariffs hit Canadian panels and paper from August. Mass timber grows ~20% a year. California extended cap-and-invest to 2045 with more offsets, yet 2026 offset issuance is historically low. [RFF](https://www.rff.org/publications/reports/will-increased-timber-harvesting-on-federal-lands-reduce-growing-wildfire-hazards/), [F&W](https://www.fwforestry.com/press-release/fw-regional-timber-market-insights-summer-2026/), [CFJC](https://cfjctoday.com/2026/07/22/canadian-forest-sector-faces-another-blow-with-new-u-s-tariff-onslaught/), [IWF](https://www.iwfatlanta.com/industry-trends/2025-was-a-pivotal-year-for-mass-timber-construction), [ICAP](https://icapcarbonaction.com/en/news/california-extends-cap-and-trade-2045-renames-program-cap-and-invest), [ClimeCo](https://www.climeco.com/newsroom/offset-pricing-monthly-market-digest-july-2026/)

## Climate, fire, water, pests
- 8.3M+ acres burned by Sept 5, 2026, a decade high. An outside analysis found fuels treatment fell from 4M+ acres (2024) to ~2.6M (2025); the agency cites firefighting demands and weather. [Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/us-wildfires-hit-highest-acreage-burned-in-a-decade-12409322)
- 2026 is virtually certain to exceed 1.5°C; a record El Niño raises odds of a record 2027. [Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-warmest-october-record-2026-virtually-certain-exceed-15degc), [Carbon Brief](https://www.carbonbrief.org/state-of-the-climate-strong-el-nino-puts-2026-on-track-for-second-warmest-year)
- Colorado River: lowest combined Powell–Mead storage since 1963; Lower Basin cuts of 1.25M acre-feet a year. [DOI](https://www.doi.gov/pressreleases/interior-department-finalizes-plans-2027-2028-colorado-river-operations)
- Insurer retreat spreads inland (Utah's 2025 nonrenewal rate led the nation). Emerald ash borer reached its southernmost known US point in Texas; sudden oak death was first found in Minnesota. [KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/2026-09-24/surplus-lines-homeowners-insurance-utah-wildfire-climate-change), [Fox 4](https://www.fox4news.com/news/invasive-insect-texas-emerald-ash-borer), [UMN](https://mitppc.umn.edu/news/biosurveillance-project-flags-first-sudden-oak-death-detection-minnesota)
