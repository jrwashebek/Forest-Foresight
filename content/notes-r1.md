# Notes r1: forestry context (Nov 14, 2026)

**Workforce**
- The Forest Service went from more than 39,000 employees to about 33,000, a 16% drop ([AP/KTAR, Oct 22](https://ktar.com/national-news/agriculture-secretary-says-agency-is-full-bore-ahead-on-forest-service-reorganization)). GAO puts the 2025 loss at about 20% ([GAO-26-107993](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-107993/index.html)). *Figures differ; cite per card.*
- Seasonal firefighter hiring beat target (11,550 by June) ([NPR/OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2026/06/18/as-western-fires-erupt-trump-s-forest-service-says-it-s-now-fully-staffed/)). Hotshot crews still fell about 15% ([NYT/GV Wire](https://gvwire.com/2026/08/14/federal-job-cuts-are-making-wildfires-harder-to-fight/)). *Whether the agency is "fully staffed" is disputed.*
- Firefighter pay raises were made permanent in March 2025 ([DOI](https://www.doi.gov/node/62046)).

**Structure**
- Planned reorganization: headquarters moves to Salt Lake City, regional offices are replaced by 15 state directors, and research stations close ([GovExec](https://www.govexec.com/management/2026/04/forest-service-move-hq-out-dc-shutter-regional-offices-sweeping-overhaul/412566)). A judge halted it on Oct 13, the ruling is under appeal; USDA presses ahead (AP, above). *Its final shape is uncertain.*
- The U.S. Wildland Fire Service launched at Interior in January. Congress kept Forest Service firefighters out of it and ordered a feasibility study, due Feb 15, 2027 ([GovExec](https://www.govexec.com/management/2026/01/trump-administration-stands-consolidated-federal-firefighting-agency-over-bipartisan-congressional-reservations/410661); [CalMatters](https://calmatters.org/environment/wildfires/2026/08/trump-wildfire-wildland-firefighting-federal-california/)).
- Both agencies now run full-suppression policies ([AP/KTAR](https://ktar.com/national-news/as-wildfires-worsen-trump-administration-revives-discredited-policy-to-stomp-out-all-fires-quickly/5884780/)). *The safety and fuels effects are contested.*

**Money**
- The FY27 request would cut the Forest Service line about 75%, mostly by moving fire to Interior. The House committee bill gives the agency about 2% more. A stopgap runs to Dec 11, so a funding gap is possible on Dec 12 ([CRS R49292](https://www.everycrsreport.com/files/2026-10-21_R49292_bfd3ba78b460c7241162661110ab1cc6e1fd19bc.html)).
- The suppression cap adjustment expires after FY2027 ([E&E](https://www.eenews.net/articles/forest-service-nears-a-return-to-fire-borrowing-unless-congress-acts)).
- The 2025 reconciliation law clawed back unspent IRA forestry funds ([NAISMA](https://naisma.org/2025/09/22/how-obbb-impacts-invasive-species-and-forest-resilience-programs/)). *Rescinded dollar amounts are unverified. I did not confirm when IIJA funds end; check before writing a card.*
- Secure Rural Schools authorization expired Sep 30, and a reauthorization bill is pending ([CWSF](https://www.westernforesters.org/sites/default/files/October2026_CWSFLegislative_PolicyUpdate.pdf)).

**Rules and legislation**
- USDA's final NEPA procedures took effect Apr 3 ([WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/client-alerts/20260427-us-department-of-agriculture-finalizes-updated-nepa-procedures)).
- The Roadless Rule rescission is proposed but not final ([EELP](https://eelp.law.harvard.edu/trump-administration-proposes-to-rescind-roadless-rule/)).
- The Fix Our Forests Act is stalled before a Senate floor vote ([Insurance Business](https://www.insurancebusinessmag.com/us/news/catastrophe/wildfire-bill-stalls-in-senate-as-insurance-market-keeps-shrinking-585530.aspx)). A prescribed-fire Clean Air Act bill passed the Senate on Sep 29 ([Roll Call](https://rollcall.com/2026/09/29/wildfire-bill-to-ease-path-for-controlled-burns-passes-senate/)).

**On the ground**
- Fuel treatments fell about 35% in 2025, and prescribed fire was roughly halved ([NPR/KPBS](https://www.kpbs.org/news/2026/05/04/trump-administration-falls-behind-on-wildfire-prevention-with-risky-fire-season-ahead)).
- By mid-October 2026, about 9 million acres had burned. Oregon set a state record, and 20 firefighters died ([KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/2026-10-16/in-lives-and-homes-2026-wildfire-season-exacted-a-high-toll-and-its-not-over-yet)).

**States and partners**
- There are 540 Good Neighbor agreements ([CRS R49480](https://www.everycrsreport.com/reports/R49480.html)), plus a 20-year shared-stewardship deal with Alaska ([WoodCentral](https://woodcentral.com.au/alaska-signs-20-year-deal-to-unlock-300000-acres-of-tongass-timber/)).
- Utility liability limits are spreading across Western states ([KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/regional-news/2025-08-31/electric-companies-are-fighting-across-the-west-to-limit-their-blame-for-wildfires)).
- Utah led the nation in home-insurance nonrenewals in 2025, at 4.5% ([KUNC](https://www.kunc.org/2026-09-17/utah-homeowners-insurance-wildfires-climate-change)).
