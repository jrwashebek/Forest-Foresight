# Notes r2: Health, Water, Climate, Wood (reviewed 2026-11-14)

**Ecology**
- Drought, heat and pests are compounding. New Mexico tree deaths tripled in 2025 ([Grant County Beat](https://grantcountybeat.com/nm-news/non-local-news-releases/tree-deaths-tripled-across-new-mexico-in-2025-amid-drought-heat)). Beech leaf disease is now in 15 states ([Maryland Matters](https://marylandmatters.org/2025/09/22/disease-threatens-beech-trees-in-all-chesapeake-bay-states/)). Southern pine beetle is moving north ([MassWildlife](https://www.mass.gov/news/a-tiny-beetle-a-big-wake-up-call-for-massachusetts-forests)).
- The 2026 snow drought set records across the West ([NRCS](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/state-offices/colorado/news/record-low-april-1-snowpack-across-western-us-means-impending-water)). The Colorado River now runs under a federal framework for 2027–36 because the basin states never agreed on one ([DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-us/insights/publications/2026/08/new-federal-framework-charts-the-colorado-rivers-next-decade)).
- Forest carbon sinks are weakening. Canada's forests crossed from sink to source around 2021 ([Corporate Knights](https://corporateknights.com/climate/canadas-forests-are-shifting-from-carbon-sink-to-carbon-source/)), and the Bartlett Experimental Forest has been a net source in some years ([UNH](https://www.unh.edu/node/2718)).
- Replanting is far behind fire losses, with seed and nursery supply as the bottleneck ([CapRadio](https://www.capradio.org/articles/2026/07/27/california-cant-replant-its-forests-fast-enough-to-keep-up-with-wildfires/)). On the positive side, APHIS deregulated the Darling 54 chestnut and the Klamath salmon runs are rebounding.

**Markets and policy**
- Mill capacity is falling nationally even under tariffs ([Wood & Panel](https://www.woodandpanel.us/news/article/us-sawmill-capacity-continues-to-decline-despite-federal-efforts-to-expand-timber-harvesting/)). The exception is the US South ([Forisk](https://forisk.com/forest-market-bulletin-august-2026-sawmill-update/)). Pulp and pellet demand is eroding, while mass timber, biochar and wood-based jet fuel are rising.
- Carbon finance is volatile. Microsoft paused new removal purchases. California extended its market to 2045, and ICVCM gave its quality label to US forest-management credits.
- Federal context: the endangerment finding was repealed, and the US stopped filing its greenhouse gas inventory and is leaving the UNFCCC ([EDF](https://blogs.edf.org/energyexchange/2026/04/15/the-university-of-maryland-steps-up-to-develop-a-u-s-greenhouse-gas-inventory-and-analysis/)). The FY27 budget request zeroes out Forest Service research and State & Private Forestry ([TCS](https://www.taxpayer.net/energy-natural-resources/president-trumps-fy2027-forest-service-budget-request/)). No cards yet; suggest the governance team.

**Uncertain**

- The Microsoft pause comes from press reports and Microsoft has not confirmed it.
- The figures for Superwood performance, ionization cloud-seeding gains and the thinning water yield come from companies or models, not field results.
- Chestnut release still faces EPA review and opposition.
